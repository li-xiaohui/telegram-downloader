# US–Korea Tariffs

Tracks US trade actions against Korea and how the channels read their real economic impact (often distinguishing alarming headlines from limited net effect).

## Mentions

- 2026-06-03 — [[brain-and-body-research-6207]] — Headline flags a 12.5% additional US tariff on Korea over forced-labor concerns. The channel's read: after the Supreme Court struck down the 15% reciprocal tariff (Feb 2026), tariffs were applied temporarily under Trade Act §122, which expires 24 Jul and is unlikely to be extended. The likely successor is a §301-based tariff (USTR has been building the case since March on overcapacity and forced-labor grounds). Conclusion: §301 effectively *replaces* §122, so little changes in net terms despite the headline. (neutral)
