# CLSA raises Samsung Electro-Mechanics TP to ₩3.2M

**Summary (translated from Korean):** CLSA raised its target price on Samsung Electro-Mechanics to **₩3,200,000** from ₩2,200,000, rating it "High Conviction Outperform." The note argues SEMCO is being re-rated from a plain component maker into a core AI-infrastructure beneficiary, with three businesses entering a simultaneous growth phase:

- **ABF substrates** — expansion of over ₩2tn expected; supply tightness and price increases seen continuing into 2026.
- **Silicon capacitors** — a recently secured order is believed tied to **Intel's next-gen AI packaging (EMIB-T)**, implying SEMCO could play a key role in AI-chip power delivery.
- **MLCC** — profitability expected to improve quickly on AI-server, industrial, and automotive demand.

CLSA likens this to the way **SK Hynix** was re-rated as an AI beneficiary through HBM: the two names historically correlated, diverged through the AI cycle, and are said to be converging again as SEMCO's AI exposure widens. CLSA raised its 2027–2028 estimates alongside the target.
