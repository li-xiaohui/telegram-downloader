# Read-through on US 12.5% additional tariff on Korea

**Summary (translated from Korean):** A headline (Hankyung) warns the US will impose a 12.5% additional tariff on Korea, citing insufficient action on forced labor. The channel's analysis argues the net impact is limited:

1. The 15% reciprocal tariff was ruled unlawful by the Supreme Court (Feb 2026); since then tariffs have been applied temporarily under Trade Act **§122**.
2. **§122 is temporary** and expires **24 July**; extension needs Congressional consent (seen as unlikely). Ending and re-imposing §122 was considered but would likely also be ruled unlawful.
3. The most likely successor is a tariff under Trade Act **§301**.
4. USTR has been building toward §301 since March via two investigations — structural manufacturing overcapacity, and inadequate blocking of forced-labor imports.

**Conclusion:** Although the headline reads like an *additional* tariff, in practice a §301 tariff would *replace* the §122 tariff, so the situation changes little in net terms.
